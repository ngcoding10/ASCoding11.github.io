# Driftfall character bodies: before | after

Each sheet shows the old bodies on the left and the new ones on the right. The rows are the Noob in 8 colours (builds from the colour), then all 112 outfits in groups of 8 (outfit numbers 0-7, 8-15, ... 104-111). The models are captured standing in the air, with the same poses on both sides.

- `close_q3_idle_*`, `close_q3_rifle_*`: three-quarter view at about 9 m (the "idle" sheets show the last-held rifle on some rows)
- `close_side_run_*`: running, seen from the side
- `close_back_idle_*`: from behind
- `distance20m_far_idle.jpg`, `distance20m_far3_rifle.jpg`: about 20 m away, the real in-game distance
